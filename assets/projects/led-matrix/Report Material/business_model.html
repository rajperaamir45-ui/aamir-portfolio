Business Model

LED and its products has captured great market cap due to sustainable and low energy displays. It is widely used in lighting, signal systems, data visualization, low level gaming etc.

From market perspective in Pakistan we see that Pakistan is mainly importer. Previously EU, US and Japan collectively were dominated in this domain, and now China is.

Unfortunately its domestic output in manufacturing is approximately 0, even in second stage manufacturing ( assembly) Pakistan has no part to plays on its own land.

This motivation usually excites big part of Gen-Z (those, who want move from Lab to market ) in Pakistan. From only idea that we have zero domestic output our business model could be this. We had contacted to local hospitals, govt. Institutions( i.e. railways) and other local customers to buy from us ( made in Pakistan digital clock and other fantastic displays).

But here is the problem, in this domain Pakistan’s import already dominates. For example, China’s digital digital clock device is already evolved, it has better quality hardware quality, fast codes, excellent microcontrollers, and way better PCBs. Its multinational companies have great competitive strategies.

So certainly there is no room for our product until we don’t go with disruptive and problem solving approach. Hence considering the situation, our Business target rural primary level schools. These school are low budget and less developed. We will offer them multiple academic features and displays for students along wireless interactive features
